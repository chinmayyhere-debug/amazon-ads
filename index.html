Amazon Ads Measurement Lab

A fictional Amazon.ca brand advertises on Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Prime Video ads and Amazon DSP. The lab answers what its marketing lead would ask.

  • Incrementality: randomized holdout, +30% lift, 2.46x iROAS
  • Attribution: four models compared against causal truth
  • Segmentation: who responds to ads, and who to stop paying for
  • Marketing mix model: R² 0.975 and a budget optimizer
  • SQL: 5-table schema, CTEs and window functions, all visible
